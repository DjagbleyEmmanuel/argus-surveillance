Argus Tactical Vision Suite

Multi-camera surveillance and recording application.

Features:
  - Live multi-camera monitoring with smooth pan/zoom and digital night vision
  - Motion, human (HOG + Haar), face, and YOLOv4-tiny object detection
  - Recording and playback, snapshot capture, and spotlight/pop-out views

Bundled with YOLOv4-tiny (80 COCO classes) for CPU-friendly object detection.
